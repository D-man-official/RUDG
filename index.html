About

A campus community for builders, learners, and future technologists.

Raiganj University Developers Group is a student-led tech community of DCIS, Raiganj University. We bring curious students together to learn practical skills beyond classrooms and turn ideas into real projects.

Our mission is to make technology approachable through peer learning, guided workshops, community events, and collaborative problem solving.
